How it works
01

Build a profile

Assemble your own corpus of research papers to match against.

02

Fetch daily

Every new preprint in your categories, the moment it lands on arXiv.

03

Match by meaning

Sentence-transformer embeddings rank papers by semantic similarity.

04

Morning digest

Top matches land in your inbox. Skim the day's reading in two minutes.

Features

Semantic search

Vector similarity, not keyword soup — finds papers you'd never have searched for.

Multiple profiles

A separate profile for every thread of your research, each tuned on its own.

Match your papers

Upload your own PDFs or search for papers on arXiv. Preprint Bot then reads them section by section for sharper matches.

Email digests

Daily, weekly, or monthly. Set your own frequency and score threshold, per profile.

Open source

GPL-3.0, built by Syracuse OSPO. Read the code, file an issue, run your own instance.
